# End-of-month / start-of-month review

One hour, on the last working day of the month (or at the cap). Out come: the client report,
next month's plan, the content queue. No money moves during the review:
proposals come out as a list and {{OWNER}} approves them one by one.

---

## 1 · Gather (agent, 10 min)

```bash
A="python3 03_ADS/pipeline/ads/ads.py --client <client>/06-ads"
$A status
$A numbers --days 30 --level ad
$A numbers --days 30 --by country
$A numbers --days 30 --by platform
$A sync-leads && $A open-leads
```

Plus `leads.csv` (counts per ad, country, outcome, channel), the month's `daily-log.md`,
IG followers at start/end (baseline in `account.md`).

## 2 · Audit (agent, 10 min)

`monthly-audit.md` → pass/fail/unknown, fails first.

## 3 · What worked (together, 15 min)

For each creative of the month, one line:

| Ad | Spent | Leads | CPL | Serious outcomes | Freq | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A3 reel | … | … | … | … | … | **keep** / **vary** / **drop** |

- **Keep**: CPL ≤ target, freq < 2.5, brings serious outcomes.
- **Vary**: wins but is above 80% of leads or freq is rising → 2 variants
  (different hook in the first 2 s, short 15-20 s version).
- **Drop**: the engine's TURN OFF rules, or 0 serious outcomes on ≥ 3× TCPL of spend.

Then by **country** and **platform**: CPL *and* outcomes. A country comes in or goes out only
with outcomes, not just on CPL (the Italy lesson).

The question that always gets asked: **is the bottleneck before or after the lead?**
If it's after (slow reply, wrong channel, zero qualification), next month you don't
buy more volume: you fix the after.

## 4 · What to add (together, 10 min)

- 2-4 new creatives for next month, from the angles that worked
  (a variant of the winner) + 1 new concept to test.
  Each piece: ID, angle, hook, format → `01-plan/ads-matrix.md`.
  `content-factory` produces them, not this skill.
- Form: change a question if L2 fails.
- Countries / language: a version in the best country's language, if the volume justifies it.
- Budget: proposal for next month with the why (see `budget.md`). If the budget
  changes, a new account cap from {{OWNER}}.
- Updated TCPL: real qualified CPL over 30 d × 0.8.

## 5 · Client report (the agent writes it, {{OWNER}} sends it)

Fixed structure (base: the case study's `final-report`, generated by `generate-final-report.py`):

1. **Period** · days with spend.
2. **One line that tells, one that proves** (in your brand's voice).
3. Totals: spend · impressions · reach · clicks · leads · CPL · CPM · CTR.
4. Day by day (table).
5. Leads by country and by platform, with CPL.
6. Lead **outcomes** (counts per outcome). If they're missing, say so.
7. Creatives: what ran, what won, what gets dropped.
8. Followers (side effect).
9. Next month: 3 lines, one decision asked of the client.

Rules:
- Only numbers from Meta or from `leads.csv`, with the source in a note.
- **No personal lead data** in the report. The lead spreadsheet is sent separately,
  only to the client who owns the data, if they ask for it.
- Costs = Meta spend paid by the client; the {{BRAND}} fee is another line, elsewhere.
- AI disclosure on the creatives restated.
- One single question to the client at the end.

## 6 · Start of month (agent, 10 min)

- `account.md`: budget, TCPL, thresholds for the new month.
- New `launch-XX.toml` (or an update) for the approved creatives → `--dry-run`
  → `launch` (paused) → ok → `go`.
- `daily-log.md`: a "start of month" entry with the plan for the 4 weeks.
- New lessons that apply to every client → `lessons-<client>.md` or into
  this skill. The second client should cost half as much as the first.
